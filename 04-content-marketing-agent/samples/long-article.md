# How we cut client onboarding from 14 hours to 3

Last spring a 30-person accounting firm asked us to "add AI" to their business. Before touching any tools, we spent two days shadowing their team.

The biggest time sink was not where the partners expected. It was client onboarding emails: welcome packets, document requests, and follow-ups, all written by hand from scratch.

Across the team that added up to 14 hours every week. Most of it was copy-paste from old emails and the CRM.

We built a simple drafting workflow. It pulls the client's details from the CRM, drafts each email in the firm's voice, and a human reviews and sends it.

Six weeks later the same work takes 3 hours a week. Nobody lost their job; the team spends the time on client calls instead.

The lesson: start with the most boring, repetitive work your team does, measure it, and automate that first. The strategy writes itself after the first win.
