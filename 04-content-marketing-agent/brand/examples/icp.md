# Ideal Customer Profile

## Who They Are
Founder or managing partner of a 10-100 person professional services firm (marketing agency, accounting/bookkeeping firm, boutique consultancy) in the US, UK, or Canada. Age 35-55. Annual revenue $2M-$20M. Still involved in delivery, wears the operations hat, no dedicated IT or data team. Budget for a project: $10k-$60k.

## Pains
- My team spends hours on admin and reporting instead of billable work
- Everyone says "use AI" but I don't know where to start or who to trust
- We tried ChatGPT and it was inconsistent, so nobody uses it
- Margins are getting squeezed while salaries keep going up
- I'm the bottleneck: every process lives in my head
- I'm worried about client data leaking into AI tools

## Desires
- Win back 10+ hours per person per month
- Grow revenue without hiring at the same pace
- A clear, low-risk first step with a measurable result
- Look modern to clients and recruits
- Processes that run without me

## Objections
- We're too small for AI
- It will be expensive and take forever
- My team won't adopt it
- AI makes things up; I can't risk errors in client work
- We already pay for too many tools

## Where They Hang Out
- LinkedIn (daily, mostly reading)
- Industry newsletters and email
- Podcasts on agency and firm growth
- Peer groups and mastermind communities
- Instagram and short video, casually in the evening

## Language They Use
- "drowning in admin"
- "billable hours"
- "where do I even start"
- "we tried ChatGPT"
- "I don't have time to figure this out"
- "show me the ROI"
