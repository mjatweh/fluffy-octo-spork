# Brand

## Business Name
Northbeam AI

## Mission
We help owners of 10-100 person professional services firms put AI to work on the boring parts of the business, so their people spend their time on clients instead of copy-paste. Practical automation, measured in hours saved, shipped in weeks.

## Offer
Fixed-scope AI automation projects for agencies, accounting firms, and consultancies: we find the three workflows that eat the most hours, build reliable AI-assisted automations for them, train the team, and measure the time saved. Entry point is a 2-week AI Ops Audit.

## Voice & Tone
- Plain-spoken operator, not a futurist: we talk about hours, invoices, and inboxes
- Confident but humble; we show our work and admit what AI can't do yet
- Specific over clever: real numbers, real workflows, real screenshots
- Warm and a little dry; light humor, never snark
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph.

## Words We Use
- hours back
- workflow
- boring work
- ship
- measure
- your team
- practical
- in plain English

## Words We Avoid
- revolutionize — overpromises
- game-changer — hype
- synergy — corporate filler
- leverage — say "use"
- disrupt — not our vibe
- cutting-edge — empty buzzword
- unlock — overused in AI marketing
- 10x — unverifiable

## Example Posts
Last month a 30-person accounting firm asked us to "add AI." We asked a better question: where do your people lose the most hours? The answer was client onboarding emails. 14 hours a week. We built a drafting workflow that pulls from their CRM. Now it's 3 hours. No robots. Just less copy-paste.
---
Unpopular opinion: you don't need an AI strategy. You need a list of the 5 most boring things your team does every week. Start there. The strategy writes itself.
---
We turned down a project last week. The client wanted a chatbot. Their actual problem was a broken intake form. Fixing the form took two hours. Sometimes the best AI project is no AI project.
