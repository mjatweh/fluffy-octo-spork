# Brand

## Business Name
TailorWired

## Mission
TailorWired builds custom AI automation: tailored to how each business works, wired into the systems it already runs on. We help established, owner-led businesses keep up with how the world is moving. We connect the software they already use and put AI to work on the repetitive office jobs, so their people get hours back every week, without ripping out the systems they trust.

## Offer
AI automation and systems integration for businesses that have been around for decades: accounting and back-office automation (QuickBooks Desktop and Online, Sage), connecting systems that don't talk to each other, AI assistants that answer questions from the company's own data, document processing, inbox and follow-up automation, reporting, custom AI agents, plus training and ongoing support. Entry point is a free 30-minute call, then a fixed-price plan, one workflow at a time.

## Voice & Tone
- Plain English, always. Write like you're talking to a 62-year-old owner across the desk, with respect
- Respect what they built: their business works, we make it easier to run
- Calm and confident, never hype. No tech jargon, and explain any term we must use
- Concrete: invoices, bills, inboxes, Monday reports, QuickBooks, not "digital transformation"
- Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Warm, a little dry

## Words We Use

## Words We Avoid
- revolutionize — overpromises
- game-changer — hype
- disrupt — scares owners who like what they built
- leverage — say "use"
- synergy — corporate filler
- cutting-edge — empty buzzword
- digital transformation — sounds expensive and risky
- unlock — overused in AI marketing
- 10x — unverifiable
- boomer — never label the reader

## Example Posts
A manufacturer we work with was told the only way to use AI on their books was to move from QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online. New subscription, lost features, months of retraining. We connected AI to the system they already had instead. Same software. Same team. Now the owner just asks: "Who's more than 60 days late?" Sometimes the smartest upgrade is not replacing anything.
