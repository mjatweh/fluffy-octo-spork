# Ideal Customer Profile

## Who They Are
Owner, president or second-generation successor of an established, owner-led US business (often family-owned), 10-200 employees, 15+ years in business, $3M-$75M revenue. Manufacturing, construction and trades, distribution, property management and real estate, hospitality, professional services, family offices. Southeast first (Carolinas), then nationwide. Usually 50-70 years old, or a younger family member pushing to modernize. Runs on QuickBooks Desktop or Sage, Outlook, Excel and an industry program from 2009. Has an outside IT company for computers and email, nobody for process or data.

## Pains
- My office manager spends half her day typing things into QuickBooks
- Our programs don't talk to each other, so we copy and paste all day
- Getting a simple report takes days and depends on one person
- Chasing customers who pay late eats our time and our cash
- Everything important lives in a couple of long-time employees' heads
- Everyone's talking about AI and I don't know where it fits for a company like mine
- The IT guy says we'd have to switch everything to use the new stuff
- I'm worried we're falling behind younger competitors

## Desires
- Keep the business running the way it does, just with less busywork
- Get hours back for the team without hiring more office staff
- Know the numbers without waiting on anyone
- A trustworthy partner who explains things in plain English and picks up the phone
- Leave the next generation (or a buyer) a business that runs on systems, not memory
- Start small and see it work before committing more

## Objections
- We're too old-school for this; my people aren't technical
- I don't want to replace software we've used for 20 years
- It'll cost a fortune and take forever
- I don't trust AI with our financial and customer data
- Tech people talk down to me and use words I don't understand
- We tried new software before and nobody used it

## Where They Hang Out
- LinkedIn (reads more than posts, checks it on a phone)
- Email; reads newsletters from their trade association and bank
- Industry trade associations, chambers of commerce, Rotary, Vistage and peer groups
- Their CPA, banker, lawyer and IT provider (trusted referrers)
- Local business journals (e.g. Charlotte Business Journal)
- Facebook, and YouTube for how-to videos
- Trade shows and local business events

## Language They Use
- "we've always done it this way"
- "it works, I just wish it was easier"
- "my bookkeeper is drowning"
- "I don't speak computer"
- "our systems don't talk to each other"
- "I don't want to start over"
- "how much is this going to cost me?"
- "keep up with the times"
