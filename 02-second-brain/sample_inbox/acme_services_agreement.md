# Consulting Services Agreement

This Consulting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of April 1, 2026 between **Rivera Studio LLC** (the "Contractor") and **Acme Corp** (the "Client").

## 1. Services
Contractor will provide website redesign and monthly analytics reporting as described in Statement of Work #1.

## 2. Fees
Client shall pay a monthly retainer of $4,500, invoiced on the 1st and payable net 30.

## 3. Term and Termination
The initial term runs through March 31, 2027. The Agreement will automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides 30 days prior written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach with 15 days notice.

## 4. Confidentiality
Each party shall keep the other party's Confidential Information confidential.

## 5. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.
