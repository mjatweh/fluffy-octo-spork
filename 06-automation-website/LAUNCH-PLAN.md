## 1. Who we're for (and one correction)

You're right to narrow it, with one change: **target the business, not the age.**

- **Target:** established, owner-led companies (often family-run), 15+ years old, 10-200 people, still running on
  QuickBooks Desktop, Outlook, Excel and an old industry program. Manufacturing, trades, distribution, property,
  hospitality, family offices. **Carolinas first**, where the manufacturing case study gives you proof.
- **Two buyers in the same company:** the owner (50-70, signs the check, wants less hassle and no risk) and the
  **next-generation family member or office manager** who is pushing to modernize. The second person usually finds
  you; the first one decides. Speak to both.
- **Never say "boomer" or "old" in marketing.** Owners don't see themselves that way and it reads as an insult. Say
  "established", "decades in business", "the systems you already have". (It's on the agent's banned-word list.)

**Our one-line pitch:** *Keep your systems. Lose the busywork.* Everyone else tells these owners to switch
software. We connect to what they have. That's the AFC story, and it's what sets us apart.

## 3. First 30 days

**Week 1: go live**
- Turn on GitHub Pages and Calendly (`06-automation-website/README.md`). Test booking from your phone.
- Ask the AFC owners if you can name them on the site, or keep it anonymous. Ask for one sentence you can quote.
- Update LinkedIn: headline *"AI automation for established businesses. Keep your systems, lose the busywork."* Put the website link first.
- Post #1 (below). Send the warm-intro message to 20 people.

**Week 2: partners**
- Make a list of 15 referral partners (CPAs, IT companies, bankers you know or the owners you know use).
- Send the partner email (below) to 5 a week. Offer them a free call for their own office: it's your best demo.
- 3 LinkedIn posts.

**Week 3: rooms**
- Pitch the free talk to 3 local groups (Charlotte chamber, a Rotary club, one trade association).
- Start direct outreach: 10 emails a week to manufacturers/distributors.

**Week 4: review**
- Count: site visits, calls booked, calls held, plans sent, clients signed. Keep what produced calls; drop what didn't.

**Weekly goal:** 3 calls booked. One paying client a month from these calls is a good start.

## 6. Running the agent yourself (on the Mac, with your Claude key in `.env`)

```bash
cd ~/fluffy-octo-spork/04-content-marketing-agent
python -m content_agent calendar --period month --channels linkedin,newsletter      # next month's plan
python -m content_agent generate linkedin --topic "Is it safe to let AI near my financial data?"
python -m content_agent generate newsletter --topic "The real cost of typing bills into QuickBooks by hand" --offer "Free 30-Minute Automation Call"
python -m content_agent generate ads --topic "Keep your systems, lose the busywork"  # for later, when we test ads
python -m content_agent review draft.md --channel linkedin                            # check anything you write against the brand voice
```

To build the partner list and outreach sequence with the AI workforce (project 05):

```bash
cd ~/fluffy-octo-spork/05-ai-workforce

```

Every draft is saved to `output/` and to the Second Brain's `Content/Drafts/` folder for you to edit before posting.
