Straight answers

Questions owners ask us

My team isn't very technical. Will this work for us?
Yes. That's who we build for. Most of what we set up runs in the background. Where your team does touch it, it looks like the email, spreadsheet or accounting screen they already use. We train everyone, and we're a phone call away.
Do I have to replace my software?
Almost never. Our starting point is the software you already pay for, including older desktop programs. We only suggest a change if it clearly saves you money, and we'll show you the numbers first.
What does it cost?
It depends on what we automate, so we don't guess. After the free call you'll get a written plan with a fixed price for each piece, and you decide what to go ahead with. Most clients start with one workflow and add more once they see it working.
Is my business data safe?
We treat your financial and customer data the way your accountant would. Access is limited to what each automation needs, you can see what's connected, and you can switch anything off at any time.
Will AI replace my employees?
That's not what we do. We take the copy-paste and chasing off your team's plate, so the people who know your business spend their time on customers, quality and growth.
How long until something is actually working?
The first automation is usually running within a few weeks of agreeing on the plan. We'd rather give you one thing that works soon than a big project that drags on.
