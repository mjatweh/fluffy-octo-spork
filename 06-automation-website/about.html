// our story

Built inside real businesses first.

We started by automating operations inside a group of companies across manufacturing, real estate, hospitality and technology. Every one of them ran differently, and every vendor said the same thing: switch your systems.

So we did the opposite: kept the systems people trusted, connected them, and put AI on the repetitive work in between. That approach became TailorWired: automation tailored to you, wired into what you have.
